Not an AI conference. Not a data centre show.

AI InfraNext is the executive summit for the infrastructure layer beneath enterprise AI focused on the compute, cloud, power, connectivity and security decisions required to run AI at production scale.

From announced to operational: Bridging the gap between AI ambition and physical capability

As generative AI, autonomous agents, and foundation models scale into core enterprise workflows, the bottleneck shifts from algorithms to physical execution: high-density power access, liquid-cooled data halls, low-latency interconnects, and sovereign data boundaries. AI InfraNext brings together the executive leaders who build, power, secure, and finance this mission-critical foundation.
