01Overview & Scope

A single-page series gateway for AI InfraNext — Trescon's executive summit series dedicated to the infrastructure layer beneath enterprise AI (compute, cloud, data centres, power, connectivity, security, governance, and infrastructure capital). It establishes the global brand proposition ("Where AI infrastructure meets enterprise demand"), routes visitors to regional edition microsites, and converts sponsors and qualified buyers through HubSpot.

ItemSpecification
Page typeStatic single-page series hub (index.html) + central resource/insights directory (/insights/)
Primary goalsRoute buyers and sponsors to regional editions (Explore Editions), capture series sponsorship enquiries (Why Sponsor → Enquire Now), and enforce the two-door qualification model
AudienceEnterprise CIOs/CTOs/CISOs/Heads of Infrastructure, Hyperscalers & Neoclouds, Data Centre & Network Operators, Government & Regulators, Infrastructure Capital
Supported devicesDesktop ≥ 1280px, laptop, tablet (768–1024px), mobile 320–430px (tested at 320×680, 360×800, 375×812, 412×915)
BrowsersCurrent Chrome, Edge, Safari (macOS/iOS), Firefox, Samsung Internet
Out of scopeIndividual edition microsites (/indonesia/, /malaysia/, /singapore/, /uae/) — maintained as dedicated edition projects routed via Cloudflare Worker

02Hosting Architecture

aiinfranext.com runs natively on Cloudflare Pages with a Worker router mapping regional edition prefixes (/indonesia/*, /malaysia/*, etc.) to their respective edition Pages projects while serving the series homepage and global assets from the root project.

Visitoraiinfranext.com/*
→
Cloudflare Zoneaiinfranext.com · account “Trescon”
→
Worker aiinfranext-routerprefix → edition project map
→
Pages aiinfranext-series/ · /assets/* · /insights/*
or
Edition Projects/indonesia/ · /malaysia/ · /uae/…
RouteServed by
/ (series homepage)Pages project aiinfranext-series → index.html
/assets/*Pages project aiinfranext-series (global logos, media, stylesheets)
/insights/*Pages project (series thought leadership, market briefs, and whitepapers)
/indonesia/*, /malaysia/*, /singapore/*, /uae/*Dedicated regional edition Pages projects mapped via Worker prefix
Architecture rule: Never mix edition-specific registration forms (Konfhub) onto the global series root. All delegate applications and Business Pass checkouts happen on the respective regional edition microsites.

05Build & Deployment

Local preview

git pull
npx serve .        # root-relative /assets/ paths require a local HTTP server

Deploy

Push to main. The GitHub-connected Cloudflare Pages project aiinfranext-series builds and publishes automatically in ~60 seconds. A failed build retains the previous live deployment.

# Pages build command (Settings → Builds)
mkdir -p dist && cp -R index.html assets insights _redirects dist/
# Output directory: dist
Any new top-level file or folder must be explicitly added to the build command, otherwise it will be omitted from dist/. Keep all favicons inside /assets/img/.

14QA & Validation

  • Automated Copy & Compliance Audit: validate-series.js scans rendered HTML for banned strings ("By invitation only", "AI Infranext", "AI Infra Next", "AIN", "AIIN", unlabelled capacity metrics, or sponsorship prices) and verifies HTML tag balance.
  • Viewport Audit: Headless Chrome verification at 320×680, 360×800, 375×812, 412×915, 768×1024, and 1440×900 — zero horizontal scroll, no clipped text, and full CTA visibility inside sticky stages.
  • Interactive & Form Checks: Region/edition tab switching and swipe gestures; HubSpot series enquiry submission; mobile drawer open/close and anchor offset accuracy; reduced-motion fallback check.

15Conventions & Guardrails (Mandatory)

Derived directly from the AI InfraNext Global Messaging & Commercial Execution Reference. Breaking any of these rules invalidates the build:

  • Typography & Naming: Always write AI InfraNext as a closed-up compound with an internal capital N. Never write "AI Infranext", "AI Infra Next", or "AI INFRANEXT" in body text. Never abbreviate to AIN, AIIN, or InfraNext alone. Never place "the" before AI InfraNext. Use UK English spellings and sentence case throughout.
  • Core Brand Asset Separation: Keep the four verbal assets distinct — Descriptor ("The infrastructure beneath enterprise AI"), Commercial Proposition ("Where AI infrastructure meets enterprise demand"), Brand Line ("[Market]'s AI build, decided here"), and Thesis ("From announced to operational"). Never blend them into one sentence.
  • Two-Door Attendance Rule: Never use "By invitation only" anywhere on the site. "By invitation" must always travel with the qualified audience it applies to (senior enterprise, public-sector, and selected infrastructure-capital delegates classified by mandate).
  • World AI Show Lineage Rule: Never describe AI InfraNext as a track, stream, or spin-off of World AI Show. World AI Show credentials (48 editions, 4 continents, 42,000+ C-level executives) appear strictly in Section 09 as Trescon's organisational track record.
  • Sovereignty Guardrail: Sovereignty is an agenda theme (Pillar 3), never the series identity or headline posture. Frame markets as domestic enterprise and national capability building trusted infrastructure together with the global technology ecosystem.
  • Capacity & Commercial Confidentiality: Every infrastructure capacity figure displayed on edition cards must carry an explicit status label (operational, secured, contracted, announced, planned, targeted) and plain-text source (never a hyperlink). Never display sponsorship pricing on the website, and never list prospective sponsors before contracts are signed.
  • Consent Qualifier: Any mention of delegate lists or profiles in sponsorship copy must explicitly include the consent qualifier.
